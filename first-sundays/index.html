First Sundays

SLOWING DOWN
AND SHARING LIFE TOGETHER

We believe that faith is something we live out in community.

When we look at the life of Jesus, so much of His ministry and relationship-building happened around a dinner table. By slowing down once a month to eat together, we practice hospitality, build deeper friendships, and create a space where everyone truly belongs.

That's why, on the first Sunday of every month, we do things a little differently.

Some months, we intentionally slow down, pull up extra chairs, and share a meal together during our normal church time. Other months, we enjoy a meal after our gathering together.

What to expect

A table for community.

A Relaxed Atmosphere

We trade the rush of the week for meaningful conversation and connection.

Food for Everyone

Whether it's a bring and share or a simple brunch, there's always plenty of food to go around.

Community Focus

This is a space to meet new people, catch up with old friends, laugh, live out our faith and worship together, and simply be together as a church family.

The details

Join us on the first Sunday.

When

The first Sunday of every month at 9AM

Where

In the hall at Generation Schools, Imhoff.

Who

Everyone is welcome! Regulars, visitors, families, singles - there is a seat at the table for you.

Cost

Either free, or a small fee for a brunch-styled meal
