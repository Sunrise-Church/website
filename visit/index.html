What to expect

More ways to connect

Parking

There's parking available at Generation School. Look for the Sunday team when you arrive and they will point you in the right direction.

Arrival

You don't need to know the layout before you come. We'll help you find coffee, the gathering space, bathrooms, and kids check-in.

Coffee

Come a few minutes early and grab a coffee. It's an easy way to meet someone before the gathering starts.

Kids Church

Children are welcome at Sunrise. Our team will help parents understand where kids go, how check-in works, and what happens during the gathering.

Toddlers & Wiggle Room

If your little one needs space to move, feed, settle, or make a little noise, we will help you find the best place for your family.

The Gathering

Expect a relaxed atmosphere, worship, prayer, and Bible teaching. You can participate at your own pace.
