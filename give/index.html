Why We Give

Giving is an act of worship and trust in God. At Sunrise, we give freely and with joy, not out of pressure, but from grateful hearts. Your giving supports the life of the church, strengthens ministry, and reaches people with the message of Jesus. Our budget comes entirely from the tithes and offerings of our partners and all those who call Sunrise home. We aim to give at least 10% of our budget away to mission and outreach work both locally and abroad. We also handle all financial affairs with full transparency and wisdom, with no one person controlling our accounts. Together, we steward what God has entrusted to us and trust Him to provide for our every need.
