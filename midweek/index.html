Midweek

COMMUNITY HAPPENS
BETWEEN SUNDAYS

Faith is a daily journey, and true community is not meant to be contained within a single day of the week.

While gathering as a larger church family on Sunday mornings is vital, we believe that faith is a daily journey. True community isn't meant to be contained within a single day of the week.

That's why we place a high value on our weeknight gatherings. Throughout the week, we open our doors, our homes, and our schedules to connect in smaller, more intentional spaces.

Whether it's a living room or a local coffee shop, these midweek spaces are where real life, deep discipleship, and lasting friendships happen.

Why we gather

The heart behind why we gather.

To Slow Down & Refuel

The modern week can be demanding and exhausting. Our midweek gatherings offer a crucial spiritual pitstop: a space to pause, refocus on God, and carry each other's burdens.

To Build Authentic Belonging

It can be hard to truly know someone or be known in a large Sunday service. Weeknight spaces allow us to ask honest questions, share our struggles, and celebrate life's joys together.

To Live Out the "One Anothers"

The New Testament is full of commands to love, serve, encourage, and pray for one another. Our midweek groups are practical environments where we put those commands into action.

What these gatherings look like

Different spaces for different seasons.

We offer a variety of midweek spaces designed to fit different schedules, seasons of life, and spiritual needs.

Small Groups

These are the heartbeat of our church community. Meeting in various homes on different weeknights, these groups dive deeper into Scripture, sometimes share a meal, and connect and minister to each other.

Prayer Connection Nights

Every Wednesday evening from 6-7PM we gather to seek God's heart. We pray for our church, our city, and for one another, believing that prayer changes things and aligns our hearts with His.

Early Morning Men's Coffees

As men, we meet once a month nice and early for coffee and connection. This takes place on the first Thursday of every month from 7-8AM at various coffee shops in the valley.

...And Many Other Spaces

From specialized short-term courses to casual community hangouts, there's always a midweek rhythm you can plug into.

Find your space.

You weren't meant to navigate life or faith alone. No matter your schedule or stage of life, there is a midweek rhythm waiting for you. Ready to get connected?
