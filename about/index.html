Who we are

A HEART FOR
THE SOUTH

Sunrise Church is a growing community of people who are journeying with Jesus and courageously trusting Him to do the extraordinary.

Sunrise Church launched in 2023 in the Deep South in Cape Town. We gather every Sunday at Generation School in Imhoff. We're beautifully positioned between Kommetjie, Ocean View, Imhoff, and other surrounding suburbs.

Our heart is for all people to feel like they belong to our church family, and to find purpose and significance in their lives. As you walk into Sunrise, you'll find a casual atmosphere, great coffee, and an authentic heart.

"Come just as you are."
Beliefs

What we believe

"We believe Jesus is the only Son of God, who came to earth as a man, died for our sins, and came back to life again."

"We believe all who in faith trust Him as their Lord and Saviour to forgive their sins are part of the worldwide body of Christ."

Our Pastor

Tristan & Emma Jensen Lead Pastors & Olive

Sunrise is led by Tristan Jensen who along with his wife Emma, and daughter Olive, have a passion to connect with Kommetjie, Ocean View and beyond with the life-transforming message of Jesus. They value community and seek to live out an authentic faith. The Jensen family love being outdoors, enjoying all that the Deep South has to offer.
